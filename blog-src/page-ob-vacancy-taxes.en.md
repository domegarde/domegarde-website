**What it means for you:** Costs money. A fine, a refund, a tax bill or lost rent.

## What it is

Update the occupancy declaration. A furnished flat empty on 1 January owes the second-home tax. An unfurnished one empty for a year in a vacancy-tax commune owes 17% of its rental value, 34% the year after — unless the vacancy was involuntary. From the 2027 tax year one tax replaces the two: a home empty for one year in a high-demand commune pays 17%, then 34% (a council may raise this to 30% and 60%); elsewhere the tax applies after two years, only if the council has introduced it (up to 50%). It does not apply if the home was lived in for more than 90 consecutive days during the vacancy period, or if the vacancy is beyond your control. Next time you let, the relet rent ceiling applies.

## Why it exists

Empty homes in tight markets are taxed on purpose.

## What it costs and how long it takes

- **Time:** Before 1 January.

## What happens if you skip it

The tax arrives with the new year.

## The law behind it

CGI art. 232 and 1407 bis until 2026, replaced by art. 1406 bis from the 2027 tax year (LF 2026 art. 108, loi 2026-103 of 19 February 2026)
