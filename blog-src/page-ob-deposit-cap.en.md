**What it means for you:** Costs money. A fine, a refund, a tax bill or lost rent.

## What it is

One month's rent excluding charges for an unfurnished let, two months for a furnished one, none at all for a bail mobilité.

## Why it exists

A cap, not a norm — you can ask for less.

## Who gives it to you

Worked out by Domegarde from the rent and the lease type.

## What happens if you skip it

The excess is repayable and it is a bad start. No deposit is allowed where rent is paid quarterly in advance.

## The law behind it

art. 22 and 25-6 loi 89-462
