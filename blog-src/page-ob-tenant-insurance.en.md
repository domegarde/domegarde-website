**What it means for you:** Weakens you in a dispute. Not enforced by anyone until something goes wrong, then it decides who wins.

## What it is

Proof the tenant holds insurance covering at least fire, explosion and water damage.

## Why it exists

So the damage they cause is paid for by an insurer rather than argued over.

## Who gives it to you

The tenant, at the key handover and every year on request.

## What it costs and how long it takes

- **Cost:** Nothing to you.
- **Time:** Ask for it with the keys.

## What happens if you skip it

Send a formal notice. After a month you may either terminate under the lease clause, or insure them yourself and add the premium plus up to 10% to the rent — but choosing the second bars the first.

## The law behind it

art. 7 g) and 7-1 loi 89-462
