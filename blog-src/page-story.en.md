## The problem we saw

Many private landlords in France own a single home and learn the rules by getting them wrong. The duties sit in several laws, change often and carry penalties that are easy to miss. A rent revision you do not claim within a year is lost for good. A home with a poor energy label can stop being lettable.

## What we are building

Domegarde works out which legal obligations apply to a property, ranks them by consequence, and keeps the documents and dated proof that satisfy each one. It is in early access. For what is live today, what is in development and what is planned, see [Domegarde in brief](facts.html).

## How we work

- **Every rule shows its source and the day we last checked it.** Our news posts say what we do not know instead of guessing.
- **We say what is live and what is not.** The pages "What's coming" and "Domegarde in brief" keep that list honest.
- **We are not lawyers.** Domegarde gives information, not legal or tax advice.
- **Your data stays in the Paris region.** It is held by Supabase in the Paris region, and this website loads no third-party scripts, fonts or trackers.
