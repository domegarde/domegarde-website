**What it means for you:** Weakens you in a dispute. Not enforced by anyone until something goes wrong, then it decides who wins.

## What it is

The tenant: the annual boiler service, unblocking drains, a broken window pane, taps and seals, small paint touch-ups. You: anything worn out by age, a defect, a construction fault or a storm, and all major works — a boiler replacement, tired walls, the roof.

## Why it exists

The dividing line is fixed by decree. A clause pushing everything onto the tenant is void.

## What happens if you skip it

A tenant who pays for your repair recovers it; a landlord who refuses their repair can face a rent reduction and an order to do the work.

## The law behind it

art. 6 and 7 d) loi 89-462; décret 87-712
