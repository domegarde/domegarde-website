**What it means for you:** Required, nothing enforces it. Do it because it is right and it is cheap, not because anyone will check.

## What it is

Not the whole règlement — the parts on what the building is for, how private and common areas may be used, and how charges are split.

## Why it exists

The tenant is bound by them and has to know what they are.

## Who gives it to you

Your syndic, or the copy you received when you bought.

## What it costs and how long it takes

- **Cost:** Nothing.
- **Time:** Whatever the syndic takes.

## What happens if you skip it

No sanction. Without it you struggle to hold the tenant to a rule they were never given.

## The law behind it

art. 3 loi 89-462
