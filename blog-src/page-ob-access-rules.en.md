**What it means for you:** Weakens you in a dispute. Not enforced by anyone until something goes wrong, then it decides who wins.

## What it is

You may not enter without the tenant's agreement — it is their home and entering uninvited is a criminal offence. Works need written notice of what and how; none on weekends or holidays without consent; beyond 21 days the rent is reduced pro rata. Viewing clauses for a sale or relet are capped at two hours on working days.

## Why it exists

Peaceful enjoyment is the tenant's core right.

## What happens if you skip it

A year's imprisonment and €15,000 for entering without consent. Rent reduction for over-long works.

## The law behind it

art. 6 b) and 7 e) loi 89-462; art. 1724 Code civil; art. 226-4 Code pénal
