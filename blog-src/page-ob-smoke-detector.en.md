**What it means for you:** Required, nothing enforces it. Do it because it is right and it is cheap, not because anyone will check.

## What it is

One working smoke alarm, marked NF EN 14604, on the ceiling away from the kitchen and bathroom.

## Why it exists

Fire.

## Who gives it to you

You install it. In an unfurnished let the tenant then maintains it and changes the battery; in a furnished let, you do.

## What it costs and how long it takes

- **Cost:** €6–30.
- **Time:** Ten minutes.

## What happens if you skip it

Nothing in the text — there is no fine, and an insurer can't refuse a fire claim over it. Fit one anyway.

## The law behind it

L142-1 to L142-4 CCH; NF EN 14604
