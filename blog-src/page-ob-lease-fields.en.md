**What it means for you:** Weakens you in a dispute. Not enforced by anyone until something goes wrong, then it decides who wins.

## What it is

Inside the lease itself: the surface habitable, the TV and internet reception line, the energy class, the rent-revision clause and its IRL quarter, the last tenant's rent where the relet ceiling applies, the reference rents in a rent-cap area, and the permis de louer reference where one exists.

## Why it exists

These are lines in the contract, not annexes. Miss the revision clause and you can never index the rent for the life of the lease.

## Who gives it to you

You, filling in the template.

## What it costs and how long it takes

- **Cost:** Nothing.
- **Time:** Part of the hour above.

## What happens if you skip it

A missing revision clause is permanent. A missing surface exposes you to the 5% rule. Missing reference rents let the tenant contest the rent.

## The law behind it

art. 3 loi 89-462; annexes to décret 2015-587
