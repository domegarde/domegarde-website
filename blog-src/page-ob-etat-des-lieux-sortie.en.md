**What it means for you:** Costs money. A fine, a refund, a tax bill or lost rent.

## What it is

The same room-by-room record, at the keys' return, compared line by line with the entry one.

## Why it exists

The comparison is what justifies any deduction from the deposit — less normal wear and tear.

## Who gives it to you

You and the tenant. A commissaire de justice if one of you refuses.

## What it costs and how long it takes

- **Cost:** Nothing, or €160–185.
- **Time:** An hour.

## What happens if you skip it

Without it the deposit is due in full within a month.

## The law behind it

art. 3-2 and 22 loi 89-462; décret 2016-382
