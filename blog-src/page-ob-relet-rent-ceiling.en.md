**What it means for you:** Costs money. A fine, a refund, a tax bill or lost rent.

## What it is

In a zone tendue, the rent for a new tenant may not exceed what the last tenant paid, adjusted by the IRL, unless you did works worth at least half a year's rent or the old rent was manifestly under market.

## Why it exists

To stop rents jumping between tenants in the tightest markets.

## Who gives it to you

You — compare the new rent with the last lease.

## What it costs and how long it takes

- **Cost:** Nothing.
- **Time:** Five minutes.

## What happens if you skip it

The tenant can claim the excess back for three years. For an F or G property no increase at all is allowed. A first-ever letting, or one after 18 months empty, is exempt.

## The law behind it

décret 2017-1198, renewed by décret 2026-644 to 31 July 2027
