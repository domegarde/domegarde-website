**What it means for you:** Costs money. A fine, a refund, a tax bill or lost rent.

## What it is

The tenant may be charged only for the visit, the file, drafting the lease and the entry inventory, and never more than you pay — capped at €12.10 per m² in the tightest zones, €10.09 in zones tendues, €8.07 elsewhere, plus €3.03 per m² for the inventory.

## Why it exists

Fee abuse was routine before 2014.

## Who gives it to you

The agent — but you are the one letting.

## What it costs and how long it takes

- **Cost:** Your own share is negotiable.

## What happens if you skip it

The tenant recovers any overcharge; the DGCCRF fines the agent. Never a fee for the exit inventory, a reminder or a receipt.

## The law behind it

art. 5 loi 89-462; décret 2014-890; caps revalued 1 January 2026
