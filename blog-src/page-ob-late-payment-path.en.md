**What it means for you:** Costs money. A fine, a refund, a tax bill or lost rent.

## What it is

No penalty clause is enforceable. The route is a commandement de payer served by a commissaire de justice, six weeks for the tenant to pay, then court — with the préfet notified at least six weeks before any hearing. Between 1 November and 31 March nobody can be evicted, though the case and the rent continue.

## Why it exists

The law protects the home, then the landlord, in that order.

## Who gives it to you

A commissaire de justice, then the juge des contentieux de la protection. The ADIL will explain the steps free.

## What it costs and how long it takes

- **Cost:** About €150–250 for the commandement.
- **Time:** Months. Start the paperwork on the first missed month, not the third.

## What happens if you skip it

Every month of delay is a month you will probably never recover.

## The law behind it

art. 4 and 24 loi 89-462; L412-6 CPCE
