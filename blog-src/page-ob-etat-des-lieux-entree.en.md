**What it means for you:** Weakens you in a dispute. Not enforced by anyone until something goes wrong, then it decides who wins.

## What it is

A room-by-room record of the property's condition, signed by both of you when the keys change hands, with photos.

## Why it exists

It is the only thing that lets you charge for damage at the end. Without one, the law presumes the tenant received the place in good repair — and if the tenant blocked it, that presumption drops.

## Who gives it to you

You and the tenant together. If either refuses, a commissaire de justice does it for about €160–185, split half each with the tenant's share capped.

## What it costs and how long it takes

- **Cost:** Nothing, or €160–185.
- **Time:** An hour for a small flat. Do the meter readings.

## What happens if you skip it

Deductions from the deposit become almost impossible to defend.

## The law behind it

art. 3-2 loi 89-462; décret 2016-382; art. 1731 Code civil
