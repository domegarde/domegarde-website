## What this calendar does not cover

It is a short list of the dates a landlord is most likely to miss, not the full law. It does not know your lease, your commune or your tax situation. Domegarde works out which of more than 40 legal checks apply to each of your properties: see [Domegarde in brief](facts.html). This calendar gives information, not legal advice, and each line links to its source so you can check it.
