**What it means for you:** Stops you. You cannot lawfully let, or a lease or notice fails.

## What it is

Twenty-five protected characteristics — origin, sex, family situation, pregnancy, appearance, name, address, bank, health, disability, orientation, age, opinions, language, nationality, religion, and more.

## Why it exists

Refusing a tenant on any of them is a crime, and the burden of proof shifts to you once the candidate shows facts. Testing by associations is admissible.

## Who gives it to you

You. Decide your income rule before you advertise — three times the rent is common — write it down, and apply it to everyone. Never "permanent contract only for foreigners". Never refuse Visale.

## What it costs and how long it takes

- **Cost:** Nothing.
- **Time:** Five minutes, once.

## What happens if you skip it

Three years' imprisonment and €45,000.

## The law behind it

art. 225-1 and 225-2 Code pénal; art. 1 loi 89-462
