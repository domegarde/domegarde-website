**What it means for you:** Weakens you in a dispute. Not enforced by anyone until something goes wrong, then it decides who wins.

## What it is

A statement of the natural, mining, technological, radon, noise and pollution risks the address is exposed to.

## Why it exists

Almost everywhere qualifies for something — every seismic zone from 2 up, radon level 3, any prescribed risk plan, airport noise, coastal retreat. All of Paris is covered.

## Who gives it to you

You, free, in ten minutes at errial.georisques.gouv.fr. Type the address and it fills itself in.

## What it costs and how long it takes

- **Cost:** Nothing.
- **Time:** Ten minutes — but do it at signing, because it is only valid for six months.

## What happens if you skip it

The tenant can seek to cancel the lease or reduce the rent. Rare in practice; pointless to risk for something free.

## The law behind it

L125-5 Code de l'environnement — dated under 6 months at signature
