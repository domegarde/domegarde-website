**What it means for you:** Costs money. A fine, a refund, a tax bill or lost rent.

## What it is

The cotisation foncière des entreprises — a small local business tax that a furnished letting owes from its second year.

## Why it exists

Because furnished letting is a business.

## Who gives it to you

The tax office bills you in December. File form 1447-C by 31 December of your first year.

## What it costs and how long it takes

- **Cost:** Typically €150–500 a year; nothing if receipts are under €5,000.

## What happens if you skip it

It arrives whether you expected it or not. This is the bill that ambushes first-time furnished landlords.

## The law behind it

CGI art. 1447 and 1647 D
