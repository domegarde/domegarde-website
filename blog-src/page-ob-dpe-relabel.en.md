**What it means for you:** Costs money. A fine, a refund, a tax bill or lost rent.

## What it is

A re-issued energy label, free, reflecting the changes to how electric heating is counted (January 2026, and again from January 2027).

## Why it exists

Electrically heated homes can move up a class, one more change follows in January 2027, and no home gets a worse label. If yours leaves F or G, this lifts the letting ban and the rent freeze at once.

## Who gives it to you

You, online at observatoire-dpe-audit.ademe.fr, with the 13-character number printed on your DPE. No new visit.

## What it costs and how long it takes

- **Cost:** Nothing.
- **Time:** About five minutes.

## What happens if you skip it

You go on treating the property as F or G — frozen rent and, from 2028, no letting — when it may no longer be either.

## The law behind it

arrêté 13 août 2025 — coefficient 2.3 → 1.9 from 1 Jan 2026; arrêté 19 août 2026 — 1.9 → 1.7 from 1 Jan 2027
