**What it means for you:** Stops you. You cannot lawfully let, or a lease or notice fails.

## What it is

A tenant aged over 65 at the end of the lease with income under the social-housing ceiling — €26,920 for one person in Île-de-France for 2026 — cannot be given notice of any kind, including for sale, unless you offer them equivalent housing nearby.

## Why it exists

Unless you are yourself over 65 or under the same ceiling.

## What it costs and how long it takes

- **Time:** Check before you plan anything.

## What happens if you skip it

The notice is void.

## The law behind it

art. 15 III loi 89-462; arrêté 19 décembre 2025 (ceilings)
