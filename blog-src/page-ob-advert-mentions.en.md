**What it means for you:** Costs money. A fine, a refund, a tax bill or lost rent.

## What it is

Rent excluding charges, the charges and how they're recovered, the deposit, the word "meublé" if it is, the commune or arrondissement, the surface, both DPE letters with the estimated yearly energy cost and its reference year, "logement à consommation énergétique excessive" for F and G, the reference rents in a rent-cap area, and the sentence "Les informations sur les risques auxquels ce bien est exposé sont disponibles sur le site Géorisques".

## Why it exists

So a tenant can compare honestly before visiting. Binding on private landlords since July 2022, not only agencies.

## Who gives it to you

You, when you write the advert.

## What it costs and how long it takes

- **Cost:** Nothing.
- **Time:** Ten minutes with the list.

## What happens if you skip it

Up to €5,000 for a private landlord under the 1989 law, and a separate DGCCRF fine of up to €3,000 for a missing energy label. The cheapest fine on this page to avoid.

## The law behind it

art. 2-1 loi 89-462; arrêté 21 avril 2022; L126-33 CCH
