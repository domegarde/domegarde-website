**What it means for you:** Costs money. A fine, a refund, a tax bill or lost rent.

## What it is

A furnished letting is a business in the eyes of the tax office. You register it online through the INPI guichet unique within fifteen days of the first lease and receive a SIRET number.

## Why it exists

It's how the tax office knows to expect BIC income rather than property income, and it triggers the choice of regime.

## Who gives it to you

You, at formalites.entreprises.gouv.fr. It is bureaucratic but free.

## What it costs and how long it takes

- **Cost:** Nothing.
- **Time:** About forty minutes.

## What happens if you skip it

Late registration is regularisable but attracts questions. From year two you also owe the CFE — business rates — unless receipts stay under €5,000.

## The law behind it

CGI art. 155 IV; guichet unique INPI within 15 days
