**What it means for you:** Costs money. A fine, a refund, a tax bill or lost rent.

## What it is

An exhaustive list of what you may ask a candidate or guarantor for: one identity document, one proof of address, proof of activity, proof of resources. Nothing else.

## Why it exists

To stop landlords demanding bank statements, photos and medical records — all of which are prohibited, along with a RIB before signature, a carte Vitale, a criminal record or divorce papers.

## Who gives it to you

You. Originals may be required; signed copies may not.

## What it costs and how long it takes

- **Cost:** Nothing.
- **Time:** Read the list once.

## What happens if you skip it

Up to €3,000, and the candidate can report you to the préfet within a year.

## The law behind it

art. 22-2 loi 89-462; décret 2015-1437 as amended 2019
