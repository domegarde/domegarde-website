**What it means for you:** Costs money. A fine, a refund, a tax bill or lost rent.

## What it is

On impots.gouv.fr, under Gérer mes biens immobiliers, you record who occupies each property and from when — at every change, and in any case by 30 June for the situation on 1 January.

## Why it exists

It replaced the taxe d'habitation return. It is how the tax office knows the flat is let rather than a second home.

## Who gives it to you

You, online.

## What it costs and how long it takes

- **Cost:** Nothing.
- **Time:** Five minutes.

## What happens if you skip it

€150 per property. Enforced against multi-owners in 2026 and announced for everyone in 2027.

## The law behind it

CGI art. 1418 — "Gérer mes biens immobiliers"
