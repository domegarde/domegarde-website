**What it means for you:** Costs money. A fine, a refund, a tax bill or lost rent.

## What it is

In Paris and eight other territories the rent may not exceed the reference rent plus 20% for the area, size, period and letting type, and any supplement above it must be justified by an exceptional feature.

## Why it exists

Rent control, applied by the préfet.

## Who gives it to you

You — the reference rents are published by the préfecture and must be quoted in the lease and the advertisement.

## What it costs and how long it takes

- **Cost:** Nothing.
- **Time:** Ten minutes on the préfecture's simulator.

## What happens if you skip it

Up to €5,000 from the préfet (€15,000 for a company), and the tenant can ask for the rent to be reduced and the excess refunded. The scheme lapses on 25 November 2026 unless Parliament extends it.

## The law behind it

art. 140 loi ELAN — expires 25 November 2026 (one Service-Public page says 24) unless extended
