There are more than 40 duties on this list. They come from the 1989 tenancy law and from tax, consumer and housing rules. We order them by the moment they come up in a letting, and tag each one by what it costs to ignore: it stops you letting, it costs money, it weakens you in a dispute, or it is required and nothing enforces it.

Each duty has its own page, with what it is, why it exists, who gives it to you, what it costs, what happens if you skip it, and the law behind it. This list gives information, not legal advice.
