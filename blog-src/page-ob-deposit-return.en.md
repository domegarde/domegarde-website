**What it means for you:** Costs money. A fine, a refund, a tax bill or lost rent.

## What it is

Within one month of the keys coming back if the exit inventory matches the entry one, two months if it doesn't. Deductions need proof — a quote is enough, the work needn't be done — less wear and tear. In a collective building you may hold back up to 20% until the annual accounts, then settle within a month.

## Why it exists

It is the tenant's money.

## Who gives it to you

You, to the forwarding address they gave.

## What it costs and how long it takes

- **Time:** Diary it from the day of the keys.

## What happens if you skip it

10% of the monthly rent for every month started, on the amount still owed — provided the tenant gave you an address.

## The law behind it

art. 22 loi 89-462
