**What it means for you:** Weakens you in a dispute. Not enforced by anyone until something goes wrong, then it decides who wins.

## What it is

The official model contract — annex 1 for unfurnished, annex 2 for furnished. Leases signed or renewed from 1 October 2026 use the new version, which makes a six-week termination clause for unpaid rent or deposit compulsory.

## Why it exists

So the clauses are the ones the law allows. Anything outside the template that favours you is simply struck out.

## Who gives it to you

You. The template is free from service-public.gouv.fr. A bail mobilité has its own rules and no template.

## What it costs and how long it takes

- **Cost:** Nothing.
- **Time:** An hour to complete carefully.

## What happens if you skip it

No fine. Forbidden clauses are void, and a lease without the required mentions is harder to enforce and easier to contest.

## The law behind it

décret 2015-587, rewritten by décret 2026-596 for leases signed or renewed from 1 October 2026
