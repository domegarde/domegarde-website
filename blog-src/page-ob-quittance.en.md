**What it means for you:** Required, nothing enforces it. Do it because it is right and it is cheap, not because anyone will check.

## What it is

A quittance, free, whenever the tenant asks, showing rent and charges separately. A partial payment gets a reçu, not a quittance.

## Why it exists

The tenant needs it for housing benefit and as proof of address.

## Who gives it to you

You — Domegarde's statements do this. Electronically only if the tenant has agreed.

## What it costs and how long it takes

- **Cost:** Nothing. Charging for one is void.
- **Time:** A click.

## What happens if you skip it

No sanction, but you must provide it and it is the easiest goodwill there is.

## The law behind it

art. 21 loi 89-462
