**What it means for you:** Stops you. You cannot lawfully let, or a lease or notice fails.

## What it is

Six months before the lease term for an unfurnished let, three for a furnished, received in time, served on every co-tenant with the information leaflet — and for one of three reasons only: to sell, with the tenant's two-month right to buy first; to house yourself or close family, named and justified; or a serious and legitimate reason you can prove.

## Why it exists

A tenant's home is protected. If you bought with the tenant in place, a notice for sale can't be given before the first renewal when the term falls within three years of purchase.

## Who gives it to you

You — and the ADIL, before you send anything.

## What it costs and how long it takes

- **Cost:** Registered post, or a commissaire de justice for certainty.
- **Time:** Diary the deadline. Miss it by a day and the lease renews for the full term.

## What happens if you skip it

A void notice is nothing. A fraudulent one — a sale or a reprise that never happens — is €6,000 and damages.

## The law behind it

art. 15 I–II and 25-8 loi 89-462
