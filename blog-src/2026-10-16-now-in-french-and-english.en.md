Until this week, the Domegarde app only spoke English. For a product about French rental rules, that made little sense. So we fixed it: the app now opens in French, and an FR / EN switch at the top of every page changes the language.

## What is translated

Everything you see: the login page, the dashboard and its "needs you" list, each property page, the rent ledger, expenses, documents, letters, your profile, and the tenant's own space.

That includes the compliance checklist, with more than 40 legal checks and their plain-language explanations: what each one is, why it exists, what it costs, how long it takes, and what happens if you skip it.

## What stays in French on purpose

The documents Domegarde prepares for your tenant (the rent revision letter, the rent statements and the printed record of your messages) are always in French, whichever language you read the app in. Your tenant reads French, and a letter may end up in front of a French court.

What you type yourself, such as notes and messages, is not translated.

## What we are still checking

We wrote the French legal wording ourselves, and a French speaker with legal knowledge has not read all of it yet. The compliance rules themselves have not been checked against the legal texts or reviewed by a lawyer either. Until that is done, please treat Domegarde as a way to organise and check what you hold, not as legal advice.

## Try it

The app is in early access. [Request access](get-started.html), then use the switch at the top of any page. Your choice is remembered for a year.
