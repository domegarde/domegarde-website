**What it means for you:** Costs money. A fine, a refund, a tax bill or lost rent.

## What it is

A survey of lead-based paint in older buildings.

## Why it exists

It exists because lead paint harms children. Any building put up before 1949 is presumed to have it.

## Who gives it to you

A certified diagnostician, usually in the same visit as the DPE.

## What it costs and how long it takes

- **Cost:** €90–150.
- **Time:** Same visit; report within two days.

## What happens if you skip it

Six years' validity if lead is found; unlimited if none. Without one you carry the liability if a child is harmed, and the tenant can seek to annul the lease.

## The law behind it

L1334-5 CSP — buildings built before 1 January 1949
