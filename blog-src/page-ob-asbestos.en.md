**What it means for you:** Weakens you in a dispute. Not enforced by anyone until something goes wrong, then it decides who wins.

## What it is

The asbestos file for the private parts of your flat — not the building's file. Your syndic holds a DTA for the common parts; it does not cover your flat and does not count.

## Why it exists

Asbestos was in common building materials until 1997.

## Who gives it to you

A certified diagnostician. A house needs no asbestos file to be let at all — that obligation exists only on sale.

## What it costs and how long it takes

- **Cost:** €80–140.
- **Time:** Same visit.

## What happens if you skip it

It is not annexed to the lease: you tell the tenant it exists and where to see it. No fine, but liability if anyone is exposed and you never had it done.

## The law behind it

R1334-29-4 to -29-7 CSP — collective buildings permitted before 1 July 1997
