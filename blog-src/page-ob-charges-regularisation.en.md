**What it means for you:** Costs money. A fine, a refund, a tax bill or lost rent.

## What it is

Once a year you compare the provisions the tenant paid against the real recoverable charges — water, collective heating, lift, common-area cleaning and electricity, the caretaker's share, refuse tax — and settle the difference either way.

## Why it exists

Provisions are estimates. The law only lets you keep what was actually spent on the listed items.

## Who gives it to you

You, from the syndic's annual accounts, sending the breakdown a month before and keeping the receipts available for six months.

## What it costs and how long it takes

- **Cost:** Nothing.
- **Time:** An hour a year.

## What happens if you skip it

You can claim arrears for three years — but a reconciliation more than a year late lets the tenant spread the payment over twelve months. A fixed forfait instead of provisions is lawful only in a furnished let or a colocation.

## The law behind it

art. 23 loi 89-462; décret 87-713
