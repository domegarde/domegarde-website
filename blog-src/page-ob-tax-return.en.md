**What it means for you:** Costs money. A fine, a refund, a tax bill or lost rent.

## What it is

Unfurnished: property income — micro-foncier with a 30% allowance up to €15,000 of rent, or the real regime on form 2044. Furnished: business income — micro-BIC with a 50% allowance up to €83,600 (€77,700 for income earned in 2025), or the real regime with depreciation.

## Why it exists

Rent is income.

## Who gives it to you

You, in May or June. Social levies are 17.2% on property income and 18.6% on furnished income from 2025.

## What it costs and how long it takes

- **Cost:** The tax.
- **Time:** An evening.

## What happens if you skip it

Interest and penalties. And for furnished lets, depreciation you deducted is now added back to the gain when you sell — plan for it.

## The law behind it

CGI — revenus fonciers (2044) or BIC (2042-C-PRO); LFSS 2026 art. 12
