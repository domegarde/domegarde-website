**What it means for you:** Costs money. A fine, a refund, a tax bill or lost rent.

## What it is

The living floor area, stated in the lease for every letting, furnished or not.

## Why it exists

The tenant is entitled to know what they are paying for. In an unfurnished let, a real surface more than 5% below the stated one earns them a proportional rent reduction.

## Who gives it to you

You can measure it: floor area under at least 1.80 m of headroom, excluding walls, partitions, stairs, balconies, cellars and garages. A diagnostician will do it for €60–120, usually in the same visit.

## What it costs and how long it takes

- **Cost:** Nothing, or €60–120.
- **Time:** An hour with a tape.

## What happens if you skip it

An overstated surface costs you rent for the life of the lease. Measure it once, generously in the tenant's favour, and keep the note.

## The law behind it

art. 3 and 3-1 loi 89-462; R156-1 CCH
