**What it means for you:** Weakens you in a dispute. Not enforced by anyone until something goes wrong, then it decides who wins.

## What it is

A guarantor signs an act stating the rent, how it is revised, and a ceiling they write out themselves in words and figures. You give them a copy of the lease.

## Why it exists

Since 2022 the handwritten ceiling is a condition of validity again.

## Who gives it to you

You draw it up; the guarantor writes and signs. If you hold rent-guarantee insurance or Visale you may not also take a personal guarantor — except for a student or apprentice.

## What it costs and how long it takes

- **Cost:** Nothing. Visale is a free state guarantee: every tenant under 30, modest earners, and anyone on a bail mobilité; it covers up to 36 months of arrears and some damage.
- **Time:** The same day as the lease.

## What happens if you skip it

A guarantee without the written ceiling is void. Cumulating insurance and a guarantor voids the guarantor. And a commandement de payer not served on the guarantor within fifteen days loses you the interest and penalties from them.

## The law behind it

art. 22-1 loi 89-462; art. 2297 Code civil; Visale
