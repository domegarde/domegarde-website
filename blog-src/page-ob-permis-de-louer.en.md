**What it means for you:** Costs money. A fine, a refund, a tax bill or lost rent.

## What it is

In some communes, permission from the mairie before you may let, or a declaration within fifteen days after.

## Why it exists

A local tool against slum landlords, set street by street. There is no national register.

## Who gives it to you

The mairie or intercommunality. Authorisation is form 15652, filed before signing; the answer takes a month and silence is approval. Declaration is form 15651, within fifteen days of signing.

## What it costs and how long it takes

- **Cost:** Nothing.
- **Time:** One month for an authorisation — plan for it.

## What happens if you skip it

Up to €5,000, €15,000 on repeat or if you let after a refusal. The lease stays valid and the tenant stays.

## The law behind it

L634-1 and L635-1 CCH — where the commune has opted in
