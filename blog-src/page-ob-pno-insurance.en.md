**What it means for you:** Weakens you in a dispute. Not enforced by anyone until something goes wrong, then it decides who wins.

## What it is

Landlord's insurance for a property you don't live in — at minimum your civil liability, usually the building fabric as well.

## Why it exists

Your tenant's policy covers their risks. Yours covers a leak into the flat below while the property is empty, or a wall that falls.

## Who gives it to you

Any insurer, online.

## What it costs and how long it takes

- **Cost:** €80–200 a year for a Paris flat.
- **Time:** Same day.

## What happens if you skip it

In a copropriété it is required by law, though nothing fines you. Outside one it is optional and still wise. Either way an uninsured claim is yours.

## The law behind it

art. 9-1 loi 65-557 — mandatory civil liability for non-occupying co-owners
