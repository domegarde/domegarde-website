**What it means for you:** Required, nothing enforces it. Do it because it is right and it is cheap, not because anyone will check.

## What it is

The standard leaflet on tenants' and landlords' rights and duties, attached to the lease when it is signed.

## Why it exists

So both sides start from the same text.

## Who gives it to you

You. Free to download; attach the current version. Not needed again at a tacit renewal — only when a new contract is signed.

## What it costs and how long it takes

- **Cost:** Nothing.
- **Time:** Two minutes.

## What happens if you skip it

No sanction is provided. Attach it anyway; it is one page and its absence looks careless in a dispute.

## The law behind it
