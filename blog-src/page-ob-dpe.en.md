**What it means for you:** Stops you. You cannot lawfully let, or a lease or notice fails.

## What it is

The energy rating of the property, A to G, on a certificate with a ten-year life.

## Why it exists

The rating decides whether you can let at all. G has been banned since January 2025, F is banned from January 2028, and F and G rents are frozen meanwhile.

## Who gives it to you

A certified diagnostician. Any certificate issued before 1 July 2021 is void today whatever date it shows — the old method was withdrawn.

## What it costs and how long it takes

- **Cost:** €100–180 alone, or part of the single visit that also covers lead, asbestos, electricity and gas.
- **Time:** Booked in one to three days in Paris; the report follows within two.

## What happens if you skip it

A G-rated let can have its rent suspended by a judge and the tenant's housing benefit withheld. A missing or void DPE lets the tenant claim damages or a rent reduction.

## The law behind it

art. 3-3 loi 89-462; L126-26 CCH; loi Climat 2021-1104
