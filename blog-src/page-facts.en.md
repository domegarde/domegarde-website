Domegarde is software for independent landlords in France. It works out which legal obligations apply to a property, ranks them by consequence, keeps the documents and dated proof that satisfy each one, and prepares the annual rent revision and its letter. The landlord reviews, signs and sends everything.

## Key facts

- **What it is:** web software for independent landlords letting a home in France.
- **Who it is for:** private landlords in France, including non-resident and expat landlords.
- **Languages:** the app and this website work in French and English. French is the reference language. Letters and statements prepared for a tenant are always in French.
- **Status:** early access. Domegarde is not open to the public yet.
- **Where data is held:** in the Paris region of Supabase, our database provider. This website is hosted by Vercel and loads no third-party scripts, fonts or trackers.
- **What it does not do:** it does not collect, hold or pass on rent or deposits. It is not a letting agent: it does not find tenants or manage a property. It does not give legal or tax advice. It does not sign or send anything for the landlord.
- **Plans:** three plans (Steward, Sentinel and Sovereign), monthly or annual, and no free plan. See [Pricing](pricing.html).
- **Company details:** see the [Legal notice](legal-notice.html).
- **Contact:** through the form on the [About page](about.html#contact).

## Live today

- **Compliance tracker:** more than 40 legal checks worked out for a property. An item turns green only when the document or dated proof exists.
- **Documents:** leases, certificates and other documents for each property, with expiry dates.
- **Rent ledger:** record payments, see due and paid dates, and print statements.
- **Expenses and tax comparison:** track money going out, and compare the micro and réel regimes on your own figures.
- **Rent indexation:** the annual IRL revision with the arithmetic shown, blocked where the law freezes the rent, with the notice letter in French ready to sign.
- **Tenant portal and messages:** the tenant sees their tenancy, payment history and documents. The message thread cannot be edited after the fact and prints as a record.
- **Dashboard:** what needs attention across your properties.
- **Free tools on this website:** a rent revision (IRL) calculator, a security deposit calculator and a landlord's calendar for France, free and with no account. See [Free tools](tools.html).
- **French and English** throughout.

## In development

- **AI steward:** being built to read an uploaded document, watch deadlines, answer questions from the landlord's own records, draft correspondence and re-check properties when the law changes. The landlord reviews and approves every action. No release date.
- **Letters awaiting legal review:** the notice to end a tenancy and the formal notice for unpaid rent are built but held back until a lawyer has reviewed them.

## Planned

Email reminders, finding a tenant, the end-of-tenancy process, more letters, recurring rent lines, and live public data. No dates are promised. See [What's coming](whats-coming.html).

## How reliable are the compliance rules?

Each obligation carries a plain-language explanation and the law it comes from. The rules were assembled from published guidance and have not yet been checked against the legal texts or reviewed by a lawyer. A review by ADIL, and then by a lawyer, is planned. Until then, Domegarde is a way to organise and check what you hold, not legal advice.
