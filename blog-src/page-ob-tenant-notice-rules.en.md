**What it means for you:** Weakens you in a dispute. Not enforced by anyone until something goes wrong, then it decides who wins.

## What it is

Three months for an unfurnished let, one month in a zone tendue or for job loss, transfer, first job, ill health, benefits or domestic violence — and always one month for a furnished let. By registered post, commissaire de justice or hand delivery against signature. Email doesn't count.

## Why it exists

The period starts on the day you receive it, not the day it was posted.

## What happens if you skip it

A notice given the wrong way is void, and the rent keeps running.

## The law behind it

art. 15 I and 25-8 loi 89-462
