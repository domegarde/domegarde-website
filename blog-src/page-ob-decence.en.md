**What it means for you:** Stops you. You cannot lawfully let, or a lease or notice fails.

## What it is

The minimum a home must be to let: at least 9 m² with 2.20 m of headroom or 20 m³, weatherproof, safe electrics and gas, heating, drinking water, a toilet and washing facilities, a kitchen area with a sink, and free of pests including bed bugs.

## Why it exists

It is the floor beneath every other rule. A tenant can't waive it.

## Who gives it to you

You, by looking. There is no certificate.

## What it costs and how long it takes

- **Cost:** Nothing to check; whatever it takes to fix.
- **Time:** An hour with the list.

## What happens if you skip it

A judge can order works and suspend or reduce the rent until they are done, and the tenant's housing benefit is withheld from you in the meantime.

## The law behind it

art. 6 loi 89-462; décret 2002-120; loi Climat (nuisibles)
