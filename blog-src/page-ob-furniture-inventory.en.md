**What it means for you:** Weakens you in a dispute. Not enforced by anyone until something goes wrong, then it decides who wins.

## What it is

A signed list of every item of furniture and its condition. The law also fixes the minimum a furnished let must contain: bedding with a duvet or blanket, blackout for the bedroom windows, hob, oven or microwave, fridge with a freezer compartment, crockery, cooking utensils, a table and seats, shelving, lights, and cleaning equipment.

## Why it exists

Without the minimum kit the letting is legally unfurnished — one-month deposit, three-year lease, six-month notice — whatever the contract says.

## Who gives it to you

You and the tenant, at the entry inventory.

## What it costs and how long it takes

- **Cost:** Nothing.
- **Time:** Part of the inventory hour.

## What happens if you skip it

Requalification as an unfurnished let, with everything that follows.

## The law behind it

art. 25-4 and 25-5 loi 89-462; décret 2015-981 — eleven mandatory items
