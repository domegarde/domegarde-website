At a distance

What gets harder when you're not there

01

You can't pop round

Certificates and insurance expire quietly. You may not find out until a tenant or an inspector asks for a document you can't produce.

02

The rules are French, and so is the paperwork

Which diagnostics, which notices, which deadlines: they depend on the building, the address and the lease, and they are set out in French law.

03

Formal letters still have to arrive

A rent revision must reach your tenant by registered letter with acknowledgement of receipt, or by hand against signature. From abroad, that means posting it yourself, correctly and on time.

How Domegarde helps

The same tool, wherever you live.

Know what applies

About ten questions about the building, its address and the lease are enough to work out which of 44 legal obligations apply.

Explained in English

Each obligation says what it is, why it exists, what it costs, how long it takes and what happens if you skip it, in plain English.

Proof, not promises

An item turns green only when the document or dated proof exists. You can see where you stand from any country.

Letters ready to sign

The rent indexation letter is prepared in French for your tenant, ready for you to sign. You send it yourself and record how.

A record with your tenant

Your tenant has their own portal. The shared message thread can't be edited after the fact and prints as a record.

Your rent stays yours

Rent goes straight to your own account. Domegarde never collects, holds or passes on rent or deposits.

What Domegarde doesn't do

It isn't a letting agent: it doesn't find tenants, collect rent or manage your property for you. It doesn't give legal or tax advice. Tax for non-residents has its own rules, so treat the regime comparison as a tool for your own figures and confirm anything about your situation with an accountant. The app is in English today and a French interface is planned (see What's coming).

Questions

Straight answers.

Do I need to speak French?

Not to use the app: the interface and the explanations are in English today. The letters it prepares are in French, because your tenant reads French and a letter may end up in front of a French court.

Do I need to be in France?

No. You can do everything in the app from abroad. Formal letters, such as a rent revision, still have to reach your tenant by registered post or by hand, so you send them yourself; Domegarde prepares them and records how you sent them.
