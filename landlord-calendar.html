Landlord's calendar for France

The dates that matter to a landlord in France over the next year, each with its source and the day we last checked it, and a file to add them to your own calendar. Free, no account.

Last updated: 16 October 2026

Last checked: 16 October 2026. Each date below shows how sure we are of it: a fixed date, an expected date (INSEE's usual timing), a month only, or a recurring duty with no fixed date. If a date is not published yet, we say so instead of guessing.

  1. From 1 October 2026 In force

    New model leases apply

    Leases signed or renewed from this date use the new model set by décret 2026-596 of 6 July 2026. It covers empty and furnished lets and single-lease flatshares, not mobility leases or holiday lets.

    Who it concerns: Anyone signing or renewing a lease
    Source: Service-Public: new lease models from 1 October 2026 · Checked 16 October 2026

  2. 20 October 2026 Fixed date

    Property tax (taxe foncière): last day to pay online

    Online payment is due by midnight on 20 October 2026; other payment methods by 15 October. Your own notice shows your date and amount.

    Who it concerns: Owners
    Source: Service-Public: property tax 2026, deadline to pay · Checked 16 October 2026

  3. 25 November 2026 (24 on one official page) Fixed date

    Rent-cap trial due to end unless Parliament extends it

    ANIL and the Assemblée nationale bill no. 2039 give 25 November; one Service-Public page says 24. The legal text must settle it. Until then the cap still applies where it applies. See our post for where things stand.

    Who it concerns: Landlords in the nine territories
    Source: ANIL: the rent-cap trial · Read the post · Checked 16 October 2026

  4. 15 December 2026 Fixed date

    Business property tax (CFE): balance due

    In general the balance is due by 15 December inclusive, or the next working day if that is a weekend or holiday. Check whether you owe it: some furnished landlords are exempt.

    Who it concerns: Landlords who let furnished
    Source: Service-Public: business property tax (CFE) · Checked 16 October 2026

  5. 1 January 2027 Fixed date

    Vacancy tax: a single tax starts with the 2027 tax year

    A home counts as vacant if it is empty on 1 January. One tax replaces the two old ones: one year of vacancy in high-demand communes (17%, then 34%), two years elsewhere if the council has introduced it. Years of vacancy before 2027 count.

    Who it concerns: Owners of an empty home
    Source: Service-Public: one vacancy tax from 2027 (loi 2026-103, art. 108) · Checked 16 October 2026

  6. Around mid-January 2027 Expected

    IRL for Q4 2026 expected

    INSEE publishes each quarter's IRL around the middle of the following month (the Q4 2025 figure came out on 16 January 2026). Leases that name Q4 as their reference quarter can then be revised.

    Who it concerns: Landlords revising rent
    Source: INSEE: IRL, series 001515333 · Calculate it · Checked 16 October 2026

  7. April 2027 Month only

    Online income tax return opens

    The 2027 return, for income earned in 2026, opens in April 2027. The tax office publishes each département's deadline in the spring.

    Who it concerns: Everyone with rental income
    Source: Service-Public: income tax return · Checked 16 October 2026

  8. Around mid-April 2027 Expected

    IRL for Q1 2027 expected

    The Q1 2026 figure was published on 16 April 2026.

    Who it concerns: Landlords revising rent
    Source: INSEE: IRL, series 001515333 · Calculate it · Checked 16 October 2026

  9. Around mid-July 2027 Expected

    IRL for Q2 2027 expected

    The Q2 2026 figure was published on 12 July 2026.

    Who it concerns: Landlords revising rent
    Source: INSEE: IRL, series 001515333 · Calculate it · Checked 16 October 2026

  10. 31 July 2027 Fixed date

    Cap on rent increases at relet or renewal: due to end unless renewed

    A decree of 20 July 2026 extends to 31 July 2027 the cap on rent increases when a home is relet or a lease renewed in high-demand (zone tendue) communes.

    Who it concerns: Landlords in high-demand communes
    Source: Service-Public: rent increases capped until 31 July 2027 · Checked 16 October 2026

  11. Every lease anniversary Recurring

    Yearly rent revision

    If the lease has an indexation clause you have one year from the revision date to ask. The increase counts only from your request, and it is lost for that year if you do not ask. Not allowed for a home rated F or G.

    Who it concerns: Landlords with an indexation clause
    Source: Service-Public: rent revision during a lease · Calculate it · Checked 16 October 2026

  12. After the tenant leaves Recurring

    Return the security deposit

    One month after the keys are handed back if the exit inventory matches the entry inventory, two months if it does not. Each month started late adds 10% of the monthly rent, excluding charges.

    Who it concerns: Every landlord at the end of a lease
    Source: Service-Public: security deposit · Calculate it · Checked 16 October 2026

  13. When occupancy changes Recurring

    Property occupancy declaration

    Tell the tax office on impots.gouv.fr ("Gérer mes biens immobiliers") whether each home is lived in, let or empty. In 2026 the deadline was 30 June. Check the date each spring.

    Who it concerns: Owners
    Source: Service-Public: property occupancy declaration 2026 · Checked 16 October 2026

What this calendar does not cover

It is a short list of the dates a landlord is most likely to miss, not the full law. It does not know your lease, your commune or your tax situation. Domegarde works out which of more than 40 legal checks apply to each of your properties: see Domegarde in brief. This calendar gives information, not legal advice, and each line links to its source so you can check it.
