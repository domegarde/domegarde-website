What it is

The last reconciliation of charges against provisions, and a copy of the exit inventory. No certificate or solde de tout compte is owed.

Why it exists

Loose ends are where disputes start.

Who gives it to you

You.

What it costs and how long it takes

  • Time: With the deposit.

What happens if you skip it

Nothing formal — but a tenant with an unsettled account is a tenant who takes you to conciliation.

The law behind it

art. 23 loi 89-462
