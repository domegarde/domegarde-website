Questions

Straight answers.

Is this legal advice?

No. Domegarde gives you information and reminders and prepares documents. It doesn't replace a lawyer, notary or accountant, and you remain responsible for your decisions. See our Terms of service.

Who sends the letters?

You do. Domegarde prepares the letter; you review it, approve it, sign it and send it yourself. Nothing is signed or sent for you.

Where does my rent go?

Straight into your own account. Domegarde never collects, holds or passes on rent or deposits. You record what you have received, so it can track due dates and print statements.

Where is my data hosted?

In Paris. The data you enter in the app is stored in the Paris region of our database provider, Supabase. Details are in our Legal notice and Privacy policy.
