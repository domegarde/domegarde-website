This page is a draft scaffold for the founders to review. Each dashed box marks where content goes, says what belongs there and suggests what to write. Nothing here is final. To check before it goes out:

  • Every personal fact (names, roles, dates, places, the opening story) must come from the founders. Nothing on this page about the founders is invented, and the boxes only suggest what to write.
  • Decide once whether the site says "I" or "we", and keep it the same everywhere (home page, About, this page).
  • Photos need each person's consent. Prepare them as described in the founder boxes.
  • If a story mentions a real tenant, agent or property, change the details or ask permission first.
  • The French is not a word-for-word translation: if the story is more natural written in French first, write it in French and have the English follow. Claudia reads the French.
  • The "How we work" promises are true today (see Domegarde in brief). Re-read them before publishing and remove any the founders are not ready to keep.

Example (invented, replace it):

It was a Tuesday in March when the message arrived. [Name]'s tenant in Montreuil politely pointed out that the yearly rent revision had been due the spring before. It could no longer be claimed: about €[amount] a year, gone, because nobody had said there was a one-year deadline. Domegarde exists so that nobody learns a rule that way.

The problem we saw

Many private landlords in France own a single home and learn the rules by getting them wrong. The duties sit in several laws, change often and carry penalties that are easy to miss. A rent revision you do not claim within a year is lost for good. A home with a poor energy label can stop being lettable.

What we are building

Domegarde works out which legal obligations apply to a property, ranks them by consequence, and keeps the documents and dated proof that satisfy each one. It is in early access. For what is live today, what is in development and what is planned, see Domegarde in brief.

How we work

  • Every rule shows its source and the day we last checked it. Our news posts say what we do not know instead of guessing.
  • We say what is live and what is not. The pages "What's coming" and "Domegarde in brief" keep that list honest.
  • We are not lawyers. Domegarde gives information, not legal or tax advice.
  • Your data stays in the Paris region. It is held by Supabase in the Paris region, and this website loads no third-party scripts, fonts or trackers.
