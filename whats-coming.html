What's coming

What works today, and what doesn't yet.

We would rather tell you exactly where the product stands than let you assume. We don't promise dates.

Live today Live

Compliance tracker

Over 40 legal checks worked out for your property. An item turns green only when the document or dated proof exists.

Documents

Keep leases, certificates and other documents for each property, with expiry dates.

Rent ledger

Record payments, see due and paid dates, and print statements.

Expenses and tax comparison

Track money going out, compare the micro and réel regimes on your own figures, and prepare the annual charges reconciliation.

Rent indexation

The annual IRL revision, with the arithmetic shown and blocked where the law freezes the rent, plus the notice letter in French, ready for you to sign. You send it yourself, and record how.

Tenant portal and messages

Your tenant sees their tenancy, payment history and documents. The message thread can't be edited after the fact and prints as a record.

Dashboard

What needs attention across your properties: overdue rent, expiring documents, rent freezes, unread messages and indexations due.

French and English

The app runs in French or English, switched from the top of any page. The letters it prepares for your tenant are always in French.

In development In development

AI steward

Being built to read a document you upload and pull out the facts, watch every deadline across your properties, answer questions from your own records, draft correspondence, and re-check your properties when the law changes. You review and approve every action. No release date yet.

Letters awaiting legal review

The notice to end a tenancy and the formal notice for unpaid rent are built but held back until a lawyer has reviewed them, because a faulty notice can be void.

Planned Planned

Email notifications

Reminders by email before a deadline. Today, deadlines appear on your dashboard.

Finding a tenant

The rules for advertising and choosing a candidate are already in the tracker. A candidate pipeline with side-by-side comparison is planned.

End-of-tenancy process

Step-by-step handling of a tenant leaving, and of the deposit.

More letters

Today there is one letter, the rent indexation notice. Reminders for late rent, rent receipts, insurance renewal, access notices, charges, deposit return and CAF attestations are planned.

Recurring rent entries

Rent lines created for you each month, instead of entered by hand.

Government data lookups

Energy-certificate and risk information pulled straight from the government databases.

A note on legal review

Our compliance rules were assembled from published guidance and have not yet been checked against the legal texts or reviewed by a lawyer. A review by ADIL, and then by a lawyer, is planned. Until then, treat Domegarde as a way to organise and check what you hold, not as legal advice.
