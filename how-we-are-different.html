Most landlord software starts from the paperwork: leases, receipts, reminders. Domegarde starts from the duties: which ones apply to your property, what each costs you if you ignore it, and what proof you hold.

What Domegarde starts from

  • A list of more than 40 legal checks, worked out for each property, each citing the law it comes from.
  • Ranked by consequence: what stops you letting, what costs money, what weakens you in a dispute.
  • Closed only with proof. An item turns green only when the document or the dated proof exists.
  • A rent revision that shows its arithmetic, and stops where the law freezes the rent. Try the rent revision calculator.
  • French and English throughout, with tenant letters always in French.
  • Sources and dates. Our free tools and news posts show their sources and the day we last checked them.

What Domegarde does not do

Domegarde does not collect, hold or pass on rent or deposits. It is not a letting agent: it does not find tenants or manage a property. It does not give legal or tax advice. It does not sign or send anything for the landlord.

Where we are honest about our limits

  • Early access. Domegarde is not open to the public yet, and some features are in development: see Domegarde in brief.
  • No track record yet. We have no customers' reviews to quote, so we do not quote any.
  • Not the cheapest. There are free and low-cost tools for landlords with one or two homes. Domegarde is for landlords who want a clear list of what the law asks of each property.
