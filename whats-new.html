Changelog

What's new

What we have shipped, newest first: the app (Product) and this website (Website). Dates are the days the change went live.

Last updated: 16 October 2026

16 October 2026 Website

A security deposit calculator

  • A third free tool: the last day to return a tenant's deposit (one or two months from the keys), and the 10% monthly late penalty. Rules from Service-Public, checked 16 October 2026; nothing you type leaves your browser.
  • The landlord's calendar now links to it from the deposit entry.

16 October 2026 Website

Two free tools for landlords

  • A rent revision (IRL) calculator: your new rent from the INSEE index, with the formula shown and your deadline to claim it. Nothing you type leaves your browser.
  • A landlord's calendar for France: the dates that matter over the next year, each with its source, and a file for your own calendar.
  • The IRL table behind the calculator was checked against INSEE's data and now includes Q3 2026.

16 October 2026 Website

A blog, a facts page and this list

  • A bilingual blog: what we are building, and what is changing in French rental rules, with sources.
  • "Domegarde in brief": the key facts in one place, for people and AI systems.
  • This "What's new" page.

15 October 2026 Website

A clearer home page and a billing switch

  • A new headline and an example dashboard on the home page, and real screens from the app.
  • Monthly or annual prices on the pricing page.

14 October 2026 Product

The app in French and English

  • The app now opens in French, with an FR / EN switch at the top of every page.
  • Everything is translated, including the compliance checklist and its explanations. Letters and statements prepared for your tenant stay in French.

14 October 2026 Website

A faster, safer, more accessible website

  • Accessibility fixes and an error page in both languages.
  • Stricter security settings, and fonts hosted on our own site.

13 October 2026 Website

An honest status page and legal pages

  • "What's coming" shows what is live, in development and planned, with no dates promised.
  • A legal notice, and updated terms and privacy pages.

18 September 2026 Product

Money out, and tax regimes compared

  • Record expenses against a property, by category.
  • Compare the micro and réel tax regimes on your own figures.
  • The compliance checklist now separates the property file (about the building) from the letting (about the tenancy).

10 September 2026 Product

A checklist that follows your stage

  • The compliance checklist is organised around the stage of the letting you are at, and unfinished items carry over to the next stage.

29 August 2026 Product

Letter templates and a landlord profile

  • Reusable letter templates, starting with the rent indexation notice, and a landlord profile that fills in the sender's details.

28 August 2026 Product

A dashboard, rent indexation and messages

  • A dashboard that lists what needs your attention: late rent, expiring documents, rent revisions.
  • The annual rent revision (IRL), with the arithmetic shown.
  • Messages between landlord and tenant that cannot be edited or deleted.
  • A compliance checklist that turns green only when the document or dated proof exists, replacing tick boxes.
  • Tenant management on the property page.

25 August 2026 Product

Statements to print, and maintenance set aside

  • Statements open in a new tab, so you can print or save them.
  • The ledger now shows the list of payments first.
  • Maintenance has been taken out of the first version. We want it, but it needs proper research before we build it for real.

26 July 2026 Product

Landlord statements

  • Rent statements for landlords, with an on-time or late indicator for each payment.

23 July 2026 Product

Statements for tenants

  • Printable payment statements for tenants, with an itemised breakdown for each month.

21 July 2026 Product

The first working version

  • Add properties, and record rent payments by hand in a financial ledger.
  • A mobile menu, and the first compliance tracking.
