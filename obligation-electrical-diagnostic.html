What it means for you: Costs money. A fine, a refund, a tax bill or lost rent.

What it is

A safety check of the wiring, the consumer unit and the earthing.

Why it exists

Old installations start fires. Fifteen years counts from the installation or the last rewiring, not from when the building went up.

Who gives it to you

A certified diagnostician, same visit. A conformity certificate under six years old does instead.

What it costs and how long it takes

  • Cost: €70–130.
  • Time: Same visit.

What happens if you skip it

Six years' validity for a letting — not the three that applies on a sale. Without it you are liable if the installation injures someone, and the tenant can claim.

The law behind it

L134-7 CCH — installations over 15 years old; valid 6 years for lettings
