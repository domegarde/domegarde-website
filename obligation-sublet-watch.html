It is your property and your liability.

What it means for you: Costs money. A fine, a refund, a tax bill or lost rent.

What it is

The tenant may not sub-let — and short-term platform lets are sub-lets — without your written agreement on both the principle and the price, which may not exceed their own rent per square metre.

Why it exists

It is your property and your liability.

What happens if you skip it

If it happens without consent: termination, damages, and the sub-rents are recoverable by you.

The law behind it

art. 8 loi 89-462
