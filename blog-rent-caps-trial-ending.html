Rent caps: the trial is about to end

16 October 2026 · By The Domegarde team · 3 min read · Last checked: 16 October 2026

[BROUILLON — À VÉRIFIER AVANT PUBLICATION]

Rent caps (encadrement des loyers), which apply today in Paris and some other areas, are a trial. They were created by the ELAN law of 2018 and extended by the 3DS law of 2022, and they are due to stop in late November 2026 unless Parliament decides otherwise. Here is where things stand on 16 October 2026, and what it does and doesn't change for a landlord.

What we know

  • It is a trial, not a permanent law: the ELAN law (23 November 2018) created it, and the 3DS law (21 February 2022) extended it.
  • It is due to end on 25 November 2026. That is the date given by ANIL and in the explanatory memorandum of a bill filed at the Assemblée nationale (no. 2039). A few sources, including one Service-Public page, give the 23rd or the 24th.
  • It applies only in the areas that asked for it. According to ANIL they are Paris, Lille, Plaine Commune, Lyon and Villeurbanne, Est Ensemble, Montpellier, Bordeaux, the Pays basque and Grenoble-Alpes Métropole. The reference rents are set every year by order of the préfet.

What is being discussed

  • A bill filed at the Senate on 27 November 2025 (no. 168) would make the scheme permanent and widen it. The Senate's page shows no other step so far.
  • A bill filed at the Assemblée nationale (no. 2039) starts from the fact that, without a law before 25 November 2026, the scheme will end.
  • According to a law firm, a Senate committee adopted a text on 14 October 2026 that would extend the trial by two years. We could not find it on the Senate's site.
  • In May 2026 the housing minister said the government was studying the evaluation report.
  • None of this is law yet. Until Parliament has finished, nothing is decided.

What this changes for you today

Nothing. While the trial runs, its rules still apply wherever it applies.

After it ends, we don't yet know what would apply to leases already signed. We would rather say so than guess, and we will update this post.

Worth doing now

  • Check whether your property is in a covered area: ANIL's page lists them (linked in the sources).
  • If it is, keep the reference rent quoted in the lease with the lease.
  • Follow Parliament's vote.

In Domegarde

The checklist item "Rent within the local cap" only appears if your property is in a covered area. We will update it, and this post, when Parliament has decided.
